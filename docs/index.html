✦How the Compass Navigates

Most affordability tools ask what price you want and hand you a payment you can't afford. This one runs the math backwards, the way a careful buyer actually decides: the payment is fixed, the price is the unknown.

For any candidate price it builds the true monthly cost — principal & interest on the loan, property tax as a percentage of that price, insurance, HOA, and PMI whenever the down payment is under 20% — then binary-searches the price ladder until the payment lands exactly on your budget. No estimates dressed up as answers.

Two rules of the sea: a rate is not a price (a half-point moves your max price more than most negotiations do), and PMI is rent you pay the bank for the privilege of owning less of your own house. Chart accordingly.
